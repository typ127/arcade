ARCADE

Original games in the spirit of the early-80s arcades. They run in the browser, with a keyboard or a gamepad.

PERSEIDS

A fixed shooter through ten worlds, each with its own monsters and boss, eight fighters to win, and perks and curses to catch or to dodge.

← → move · Space fire (hold for auto-fire) · P pause · M sound · Esc menu · F full screen

Gamepad: stick or D-pad move · R2 auto-fire · R1 fire · □ start · ○ pause · △ monsters · ✕ items · R1 / L1 faction (title) · Options menu

Test mode: Shift+N / V stage forward / back · Shift+G / X / P item, curse and perk demos · DEMOS and CHEATS in the menu · gamepad L2 / L1. Games there stay off the world list.

ORBIT HALL

A walkable arcade hall on a rainy night. Step in from the street, play PERSEIDS at its cabinet for the world list, or practise in the back room. Work in progress.

← → walk · ↑ door · Enter play · Esc menu · F full screen

Gamepad: stick or D-pad walk · ✕ door and play · Options menu

Test mode: TEST in the menu fills the hall with test figures and dummy cabinets (FULL HALL). Its cabinet counts for the world list as usual.
